Rex demo time: magical and automatic remote copy/paste, drag/drop, and port forwarding (e.g. open web servers running on remote machines in your local browser). Plus, showcasing some of the internal plumbing that powers this and how you can hook into it with Lua scripting.

Our goal with Superlogical and Rex is for everything to feel local. I've said before: the moment any UI shows a selector between "local" and "cloud", the product has failed. The lines should blur, and we should build the foundational technology that makes it happen performantly, securely, and beautifully.
