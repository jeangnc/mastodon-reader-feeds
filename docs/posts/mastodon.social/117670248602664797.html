Here's my second talk proposal for #FOSDEM. For the Network room.

HTTP, HTTPS, FTP, FTPS, SFTP, SCP, TFTP, LDAP, LDAPS, DICT, TELNET, FILE, IMAP, IMAPS, POP3, POP3S, SMTP, SMTPS, RTSP, RTMP, GOPHER, GOPHERS, SMB, SMBS, MQTT, WS, WSS... curl has grown over three decades from a 100-line HTTP toy into the internet’s Swiss Army knife for data transfers. Join curl creator and lead maintainer Daniel Stenberg for a fast-paced tour through the entire protocol alphabet soup implemented over 30 years of development. You’ll learn how curl’s internal architecture abstracts different transport and application layers behind a unified URL-driven interface, why supporting legacy protocols is harder than it looks, and which protocols were nearly impossible to get right. And which ones Daniel regrets they allowed to enter.
